## Caveats

- The ratio test is a screening signal, not a verdict. It covers only homes
  that sold, and a sale can reflect post-assessment improvements.
- Some neighborhood rows have bad data (one median is $305). Filter
  `assessed_total > 50000` before computing spreads.
- No tax-rate data. Any dollar-savings figure in copy must be labeled
  illustrative until `gross_taxes_due` is loaded.
