      How the Polk County Board of Review works

      The Board of Review is separate from the assessor's office. It reviews written protests filed April 2–30 and rules by May 31. Under Iowa Code §441.37, the most common ground is that your assessment is higher than your home's market value. The Board looks at closed sales of comparable homes. It won't accept Zillow estimates or asking prices.

      How the evidence packet works

      1. Find your home. Type your address. The search covers every Polk County parcel.
      2. See your comps. Recent arms-length sales scored against your home's size, age and neighborhood.
      3. Download your packet. A 2-page, print-ready packet citing Iowa Code §441.37, ready to file with the Board of Review.
